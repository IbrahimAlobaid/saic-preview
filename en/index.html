01Who we are

A youth-led initiative driven by a passion for knowledge

A collaborative space that brings students, developers and AI enthusiasts together with experienced practitioners and trainers.

The Syrian AI Community brings students, developers and AI enthusiasts together with experienced practitioners and trainers in a collaborative space that encourages learning, exchanging experiences, developing skills, and working on practical applications and projects.

We want to make learning AI closer and more accessible to Syrian youth, turning interest in this fast-moving field into clear learning paths and hands-on experiences.

04How we work

Five tracks that carry knowledge from the classroom into practice

Workshops, camps, shared experience, project building, and connections that cross borders.

01

Technical workshops

Focused training sessions to discover new concepts and tools, discuss them and try them hands-on, with room for dialogue, questions, and learning from different experiences.

02

Hands-on training camps

More structured and in-depth learning paths that combine theoretical foundations, practical application and projects, building skills step by step.

03

Sharing experience

A platform for specialists, trainers and practitioners to pass on their practical experience, including challenges, mistakes and lessons learned, bringing knowledge closer to professional reality.

04

Collaborative learning and project building

Working in teams, reviewing solutions, exchanging feedback, and developing models and applications that can be presented and counted as tangible experience.

05

Connecting and building bridges

Strengthening ties between people interested in AI inside and outside Syria, and drawing on diverse experience to widen learners’ horizons and bring them closer to broader professional practices and standards.
