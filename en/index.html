01Who we are

A youth-led initiative driven by a passion for knowledge

A collaborative space that brings students, developers and AI enthusiasts together with experienced practitioners and trainers.

The Syrian AI Community brings students, developers and AI enthusiasts together with experienced practitioners and trainers in a collaborative space that encourages learning, exchanging experiences, developing skills, and working on practical applications and projects.

We want to make learning AI closer and more accessible to Syrian youth, turning interest in this fast-moving field into clear learning paths and hands-on experiences.

02How we work

Five tracks that carry knowledge from the classroom into practice

Workshops, camps, shared experience, project building, and connections that cross borders.

01

Technical workshops

Focused training sessions to discover new concepts and tools, discuss them and try them hands-on, with room for dialogue, questions, and learning from different experiences.

02

Hands-on training camps

More structured and in-depth learning paths that combine theoretical foundations, practical application and projects, building skills step by step.

03

Sharing experience

A platform for specialists, trainers and practitioners to pass on their practical experience, including challenges, mistakes and lessons learned, bringing knowledge closer to professional reality.

04

Collaborative learning and project building

Working in teams, reviewing solutions, exchanging feedback, and developing models and applications that can be presented and counted as tangible experience.

05

Connecting and building bridges

Strengthening ties between people interested in AI inside and outside Syria, and drawing on diverse experience to widen learners’ horizons and bring them closer to broader professional practices and standards.

05Questions and answers

Common questions about the community

What the community is, who founded it, who teaches in it, and how to join.

What is the Syrian AI Community?

The Syrian AI Community (SAIC; in Arabic, مجتمع الذكاء الاصطناعي السوري) is a youth-led initiative driven by a passion for knowledge. It brings students, developers and AI enthusiasts together with experienced practitioners and trainers in a collaborative space that encourages learning, exchanging experiences, developing skills, and working on practical applications and projects.

Read the full story on the About page.

Who founded the Syrian AI Community?

The community was started by its founders:

  • Eng. Ibrahim Alobaid (Founder): AI engineer, AI team lead and project manager at Cardiac Rhythm in the United States. Has worked with local and international companies.
  • Muhammad Ali (Co-founder and Community Manager): Software engineer, and manager of technical initiatives and teams in Syria.

Who are the trainers at the Syrian AI Community?

The trainers are engineers and experts working at local and international companies:

  • Eng. Ibrahim Alobaid: AI engineer, AI team lead and project manager at Cardiac Rhythm in the United States. Has worked with local and international companies.
  • Eng. Osama Abou Hajar: Software engineer at Microsoft; previously worked on 5G network software at Ericsson. Holds a master's degree in AI and founded the GitSyria initiative.
  • Eng. Mohammad Zayn Mahrouseh: AI engineer and AI team lead at DAWMT in Bahrain, with three years of experience at international companies.
  • Eng. Mohammad Ghazal Kassas: AI engineer at Amani AI, specializing in natural language processing, intelligent systems and AI agents.
  • Eng. Yassin Abdulmahdi: Lead AI Engineer at BeSmart.ai and lecturer at Al-Sham Private University (ASPU).
  • Eng. Saad Shahrour: Founder of Pactonic AI, CTO of Karriya AI in Sweden, and Head of AI at Capital Zone in Portugal.
  • Eng. Saad Shayah: Software engineer at Basira Foundation, specializing in version control systems and software product management.
  • Eng. Hussain Saoud: Software and AI engineer and technical mentor at FICEP ME, combining data-driven innovation with enabling technical teams to build advanced software solutions.

Who is the community for?

The community serves students and graduates, developers and tech enthusiasts, trainers and experts, volunteers and contributors, and partner organizations and initiatives.

We welcome different backgrounds and experiences; there is room for everyone with the desire and readiness to learn and to give.
