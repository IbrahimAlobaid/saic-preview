Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask most about the Syrian AI Community and its activities.

What is the Syrian AI Community?

The Syrian AI Community (SAIC; in Arabic, مجتمع الذكاء الاصطناعي السوري) is a youth-led initiative driven by a passion for knowledge. It brings students, developers and AI enthusiasts together with experienced practitioners and trainers in a collaborative space that encourages learning, exchanging experiences, developing skills, and working on practical applications and projects.

Read the full story on the About page.

Who founded the Syrian AI Community?

The community was started by its founders:

  • Eng. Ibrahim Alobaid (Founder): AI engineer, AI team lead and project manager at Cardiac Rhythm in the United States. Has worked with local and international companies.
  • Muhammad Ali (Co-founder and Community Manager): Software engineer, and manager of technical initiatives and teams in Syria.

Who are the trainers at the Syrian AI Community?

The trainers are engineers and experts working at local and international companies:

  • Eng. Ibrahim Alobaid: AI engineer, AI team lead and project manager at Cardiac Rhythm in the United States. Has worked with local and international companies.
  • Eng. Osama Abou Hajar: Software engineer at Microsoft; previously worked on 5G network software at Ericsson. Holds a master's degree in AI and founded the GitSyria initiative.
  • Eng. Mohammad Zayn Mahrouseh: AI engineer and AI team lead at DAWMT in Bahrain, with three years of experience at international companies.
  • Eng. Mohammad Ghazal Kassas: AI engineer at Amani AI, specializing in natural language processing, intelligent systems and AI agents.
  • Eng. Yassin Abdulmahdi: Lead AI Engineer at BeSmart.ai and lecturer at Al-Sham Private University (ASPU).

Who is the community for?

The community serves students and graduates, developers and tech enthusiasts, trainers and experts, volunteers and contributors, and partner organizations and initiatives.

We welcome different backgrounds and experiences; there is room for everyone with the desire and readiness to learn and to give.

What activities does the community run?

The community works through five tracks: technical workshops, hands-on training camps, sharing experience, collaborative learning and project building, and connecting people interested in AI inside and outside Syria.

Each track is described under How we reach our goals.

What are the community’s vision and mission?

Our vision is to be an active Syrian community that empowers a generation of learners, developers and trainers who can keep pace with AI, contribute valuable solutions, and compete with their skills in the local and global job market.

Our mission is to provide a motivating, inclusive community environment for exchanging knowledge and experience in artificial intelligence, through workshops, training camps, hands-on activities and peer collaboration.

What is the community’s main training program?

The AI Engineering Camp: an advanced, hands-on camp of ten sessions and thirty hours for final-year students and graduates of technical majors. It takes participants from understanding AI models to building demo-ready, deployable AI products, with a team capstone project.

The curriculum, sessions and camp questions are on the AI Engineering Camp page.

How do I join the community and follow its activities?

Follow the community’s activities, workshops and camps on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.

How can I volunteer or take part as a trainer?

The community has room for trainers and experts who want to share their knowledge, and for volunteers who believe in the value of education and collaboration.

To volunteer or to train, contact us at syrianaicommunity@gmail.com or through the community’s LinkedIn page.

How can organizations and initiatives collaborate with the community?

The community welcomes collaboration with organizations and initiatives on educational activities and capacity-building projects.

For contact and partnerships: syrianaicommunity@gmail.com or the community’s LinkedIn page.

Is the community only for people living in Syria?

No. One of the community’s work tracks is strengthening ties between people interested in AI inside and outside Syria, and drawing on diverse experience to widen learners’ horizons.

Our core idea is that geography and limited opportunities should never stand in the way of young people’s ambition.
